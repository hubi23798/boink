## Target profiles

Targets are archetypes derived from the primary persona (spec §1). No cold email — every approach requires a warm intro or a specific shared context. Hook column states the opening angle.

### 1 — London fintech founder, post-exit

**Profile:** Founded a B2B SaaS, exited 18–36 months ago. Now angel-investing and managing £1–3M across ISA, SIPP, brokerage, a nominee account at the acquirer, and a few SPVs. Has a bookkeeper they half-trust.

**Why truffe.ai:** Multiple account types, currency mix, bookkeeper relationship = all three fraud wedges are relevant on day one. The SPV / nominee account mix is unaggregable by Revolut alone.

**Contact strategy:**

- Channel: Founders Forum London (member list), Antler London alumni network, or Twitter/X DM following a relevant essay post.
- Intro ask: find a mutual YC or Antler alum in common. One-line: "I'm building wealth software that gives you an audit trail your bookkeeper can see."
- Hook: APP fraud / pig-butchering if they're active on crypto Twitter. Bookkeeper accountability if they've tweeted about post-exit financial complexity.

**Timing signal:** Active on Linkedin posting about "post-exit financial admin being a second job."

### 2 — Dublin SaaS CEO, Series A

**Profile:** Running a 30-person SaaS company. Personal net worth: €800k–€2M from equity + salary. Uses Revolut Business for company + Revolut Personal for self. Has a part-time CFO who also handles personal tax. Jurisdiction mix: Ireland + UK revenue.

**Why truffe.ai:** Mixing company and personal flows is a subscription-trap and vendor-bec risk vector. Irish CGT and R&D credit complexity means the advisor's "suggest a professional" refusal is valued — they want signals, not advice.

**Contact strategy:**

- Channel: Silicon Docks slack, NDRC alumni, or direct LinkedIn from a shared Stripe / Intercom connection.
- Hook: "Your CFO can see everything your company spends. Do they have the same view on your personal accounts?"

**Timing signal:** Close to a Series A / recent fundraise — personal liquidity event approaching.

## Outreach sequence

For all 10 targets, the sequence is:

1. **Find warm intro** — one-degree connection via LinkedIn or mutual event. Do not cold-email without an intro.
2. **Send intro message** (via mutual or direct) — 3 sentences max: problem, hook, ask. No pitch deck. No demo link unprompted.
3. **Demo call** — 20 minutes. Walk through: connect an account → first fraud signal → observer invite flow. Use demo tenant (no real data required).
4. **Cohort offer** — at end of call if interest is genuine: "We have 10 design-partner slots, 50% lifetime. In exchange: monthly call and a testimonial after 90 days."
5. **Onboarding** — white-glove. Founder does the first import with them on a Zoom. Duration: 10 minutes.
